Shipping and Delivery Details

• Regions: We deliver all across India.

• Shipping Charges: We offer FREE SHIPPING on all orders across India.

• Delivery Time: Standard orders take 6–8 working days after dispatch. Orders with stitching services require 10–12 working days.

• Tracking: You will receive tracking details via email once your order is dispatched.

Return and Exchange

• Policy: No Exchange, No Return, No Refund. Orders once placed cannot be cancelled.

• Replacements: Applicable only if damaged or defective products are received.

• Mandatory Requirement: An unboxing video of the product is mandatory for any replacement claims.

Care Guide

• Cleaning: Dry clean highly recommended for handcrafted Pakistani fashion pieces.

• Washing: If necessary, gentle hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Do not soak.

• Maintenance: Do not bleach. Iron on low heat on the reverse side. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
